How do I choose the right saree for an occasion?

Explore the collection by style and occasion. Product descriptions include the design details and suggested styling where available.

Craft and fabric details are shown in each individual product listing. Please refer to the product description for the exact details.

How should I care for my saree?

Follow the garment care label. Store the saree clean and completely dry in a breathable fabric cover, away from prolonged direct sunlight.
